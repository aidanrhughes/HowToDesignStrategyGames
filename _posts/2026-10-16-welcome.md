Welcome to **How to Design Strategy Games**!

This blog is dedicated to exploring the design
of strategy games, from individual mechanics to
broader questions of balance, player agency,
and meaningful decision-making.

## What to Expect

For the time being, semi-daily blog posts in the 2-8 minute read length, 
where I dive into the nitty gritty of game mechanics and evaluate how they 
relate to decision making and player agency. I'll also do larger analyses of 
game systems as a whole - how multiple mechanics work together to create the 
complex games we enjoy. I will also share development progress on my own strategy
games in a dev-log format.
